# Blog Asset Art Direction

Use this art direction for future learning-note and blog-directory cover images. It defines the shared visual language, not a mandatory palette or repeated scene.

## Master style prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide 16:9 editorial blog-cover artwork

Create a surreal minimalist editorial illustration with an extreme sense of scale. Build the composition around one dominant environmental form or graphic field, one small sharply readable focal subject, and one repeating visual system—ripples, contours, branches, particles, paths, strata, or another conceptually relevant pattern. Keep the framing calm and bold with generous negative space, a flat graphic viewpoint, strong silhouette hierarchy, and poetic rather than literal storytelling. The image should feel mysterious, contemplative, and slightly otherworldly, like a carefully art-directed plate from a 1990s experimental art book.

Render it as a tactile analog risograph and screen print. Construct forms from thousands of small stippled pigment particles, broken dot clusters, rough screen-print marks, and uneven hand-drawn strokes. Use visibly imperfect ink density, subtle misregistration, rough hand-printed edges, coarse paper tooth, faint paper fibers, and gently faded pigment. Texture must continue consistently across the focal subject and the environment; no element should look like a smooth digital sticker. Keep the marks deliberate and readable rather than muddy, randomly noisy, or excessively distressed.

Choose a strict, article-specific limited palette of two to four bold inks plus optional black and warm paper-white. Use flat color fields and ink density—not gradients or realistic light—to create depth. The palette may change completely between articles, but saturation, contrast, print behavior, and visual restraint should remain consistent.

Preserve editorial simplicity while allowing intricate mark-making inside the major shapes. Favor asymmetry, imperfect geometry, organic spacing, and large-scale repetition. Reuse this visual grammar without copying the same landscape, ripple motif, central figure, or colors from another cover.

Constraints: no text, typography, logos, borders, watermarks, photorealism, realistic lighting, gradients, CGI, 3D rendering, glossy surfaces, polished vector perfection, stock-illustration styling, busy collage, or unrelated decorative objects.
```

## Per-asset prompt template

Append the following content brief after the master prompt:

```text
Article: <article title>
Primary metaphor: <single visual idea>
Scene/backdrop: <dominant environment or graphic field>
Focal subject: <small person, object, structure, or creature>
Repeating system: <ripples, paths, branches, particles, contours, strata, etc.>
Palette: <two to four article-specific inks, plus optional black and paper-white>
Composition: <placement, scale, and intended negative space>
Mood: <quiet emotional register>
Content constraints: <what must and must not appear>
```

## Consistency checklist

- Wide 16:9 composition suitable for a card crop.
- One dominant field, one readable focal subject, and one meaningful repeating system.
- Extreme scale, quiet negative space, flat graphic depth, and strong silhouettes.
- Limited article-specific palette; never force one global color scheme.
- Visible stipple, screen-print breakup, paper tooth, imperfect registration, and ink variation on every element.
- Tactile and intricate, but never muddy or generically grainy.
- No text, logos, gradients, photorealism, CGI, or polished digital surfaces.

## Current cover briefs

These briefs produced the first coordinated set and can be reused when regenerating a cover:

### Training GPT-2 on 10 billion tokens from scratch

- Primary metaphor: one tiny black figure at the origin of enormous irregular ripple rings.
- Scene: warm-ivory curved ground beneath a vast cobalt sky and small ivory crescent.
- Repeating system: broken cobalt stipple rings that fragment toward the edges.
- Palette: cobalt blue, warm ivory, and black.

### Routing sensitive data before it reaches the cloud

- Primary metaphor: a tiny donkey and robed traveler confronting an immense cloud.
- Scene: low rolling green landscape beneath a monumental warm-ivory cloud field.
- Repeating system: branching routes and contour paths that split, disappear, or stop before the cloud.
- Palette: ultramarine, vivid grass green, warm ivory, and black.

### Designing an AI copilot for sensitive case notes

- Primary metaphor: one small crashed propeller plane resting quietly on an enormous grassy hill.
- Scene: sweeping green terrain beneath a broad burnt-red sky, with no fire or visible harm.
- Repeating system: torn turf and irregular grass contours flowing around the impact site.
- Palette: emerald green, spring green, burnt red, warm ivory, and black.

### When product analytics can adapt the interface

- Primary metaphor: a monumental rigid palace garden reorganized by living paths and vegetation.
- Scene: royal-red imperial terraces crossed by a central white causeway and organic green growth.
- Repeating system: paths and garden contours that transition from strict geometry to adaptive branching forms.
- Palette: royal red, warm paper-white, deep botanical green, and black.
